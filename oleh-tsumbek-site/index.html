Mariupol Paper

By Oleh Tsumbek

What the Satellites Miss: Remote Sensing, Testimony, and the Prosecution of the Siege of Mariupol

Mariupol is one of the most thoroughly documented atrocities in the history of international criminal law, and most of that documentation was produced from orbit. This article argues that satellite-based evidence, even at its most rigorous, cannot carry the prosecutions Mariupol will eventually require.
